What cannabis actually does — and doesn't do.

Cannabis has real medical uses. It also has real downsides, especially with today's high-potency products and daily use. This site is built to help you make sense of both — without lectures, scare tactics, or "Reefer Madness."

Whether you're here because a clinician sent you, because something feels off, or because you just want straight information: you're welcome here. Read what you want, skip what you don't.

Who this is for

Patients whose clinician sent them here. Anyone curious about what cannabis is actually doing in their body. People who use cannabis daily and have started to wonder. People who don't use it and want to understand someone who does. Clinicians looking for a no-drama handout.

It is not: an addiction-recovery program, a moral framework, a political document, or a substitute for talking with a clinician you trust.

A note on tone

Cannabis is one of the most polarized topics in medicine. One camp insists it's a miracle plant with no downsides; the other insists it's a one-way ticket to ruin. Both are wrong, and both make it harder for someone in the middle to get straight answers.

Everything here is sourced from primary literature, clinical guidelines, or major reviews (NASEM 2017, DSM-5-TR, peer-reviewed cohort and trial data). Where evidence is uncertain, that's labeled. Where it's strong, that's labeled too. Citations are at the bottom of each page.
